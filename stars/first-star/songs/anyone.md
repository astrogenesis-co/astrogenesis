This is one of the earliest songs I started that is still here. The thing I like about this is it was definitely made from pretty raw emotion, which can be difficult when trying to balance the technical aspects of the track. Although that is why this isn't very technical sound. In fact I only realized very long after I had worked on this song that the tracks were in time, but not perfectly synchronized, unintentionally. Either way, the track is pretty simple. The main weird kind of sound going on is actually a sample of a record from a somewhat well know band, just heavily processed. Like I said, I think at that time I was feeling that song pretty emotionally but I just wanted to mess with it as I do. Even the effect is pretty simple, it's pretty much just a comb filter and an LFO. But the result is pretty extreme. Once I get that basic loop, everything else can together very quickly. Just kind of added the sporadic drums and I distorted 808 as like a lead.

The song is called "Anyone" because I think that really was one of the first times I was really kind of experiencing a sort of loneliness, or more longing for a relationship. So that was really an acute experience of longing for attention, care whatever that I did not feel like I had. So that went directly into the song.

The song is stayed pretty consistent since then, just because it felt emotionally complete at the time. It also always was one of my favorites. But recently I am kind of seeing the roughness and imagining ways it can be refined or incoporated into a bigger story
