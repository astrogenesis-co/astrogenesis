This was another song, like Song:anyone that that was done pretty quickly and felt like a pretty raw emotional translation.

Basically the kind of weird "vocalish" sound on top came from another session where I once again was messing with a pop song just heavily processing it. This one if even more heavy and resequenced and all that than Anyone so it's way out there. Don't think it's a huge deal to say the song but I think it might just be better not to for legal purposes. The main technique I used was pretty much just using a bunch of I believe bandpass filters and adding LFOs so they are kind of moving around somewhat in sync with one another. Then some reversing and chopping of the result.

The name actually comes from a different part of that sample. After the processing, there was one part that sounded like a voice saying "Bury the sun". I didn't end up using that in this song but kept that for the name, because it is pretty spook and fits the vibe of the song.

Once I took the parts of that which I liked, I put the chords under them. A very simple chord progression, but added the distortion to make it fit better. Then the drums were also really a really simple sort of one shot try.

I think the idea behind this song kind of speaks for itself. It is dark, kind of creepy. Was made during a bit of a darker period so it makes sense.
