AI written example

It started as an accident. Heavily processed audio — I don't remember what the source
even was — that resolved into something sounding like the words _I found you there_, and
the words were the song before anything else was.

At the time I was pushing against anything that felt plain. The vocal chops were supposed
to be more of that, and instead they turned it into something close to a conventional song
that I actually liked. I couldn't have done that on purpose. That's the first thing that
moved: it stopped being an exercise in processing and became a song I thought someone
could get all the way through.

What closed it was a vocal I'd been sitting on for years — IJCSLY — dropped in at the end.
That brought the whole thing around, and it's the reason the song means what it means now
rather than what it meant in 2020.

Where it sits now is a transition. _I Can't Stop Loving You_ as haunting, as grief — and
the switch out of that into being better in tune with the infinite. God's love inside
romantic love. The song is the turn between those two, not either one of them.
