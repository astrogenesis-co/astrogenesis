This is probably one of the less complete ideas that is a named "Song". Really, this is one of the few ones that is truly built around just a melody, though there are some pretty key production elements. Essentially the story is though that I really did like that primary melody. And I kind of started to write some lyrics to it, and the phrase "Because you're you..." kind of popped into my head as the first line. Essentially thinking of this idea of just relationships, connections that go beyond any rational or normal consideration, and kind of what that means. Like some people are just cosmically bound to one another. Especially if you consider the concept of a soul. Like you can always reduce relationships to programming, and proximity and all that. But when there is a more signficant connection, just the idea of each of having this one life where that is the soul you get intertwined with is interesting to me.
