- This is probably one of the less complete ideas that is a named "Song." Really, this is one of the few that is truly built around just a melody, though there are some pretty key production elements.
- Essentially, the story is that I really did like that primary melody. I kind of started to write some lyrics to it, and the phrase "Because you're you..." popped into my head as the first line.
- I was thinking of this idea of relationships and connections that go beyond any rational or normal consideration, and kind of what that means. Like some people are just cosmically bound to one another. Especially if you consider the concept of a soul.
- Like, you can always reduce relationships to programming and proximity and all that. But when there is a more significant connection, just the idea of each of us having this one life where that is the soul you get intertwined with is interesting to me.
