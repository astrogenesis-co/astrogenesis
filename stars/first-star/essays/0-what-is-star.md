_Draft 1_

I am falling toward a black hole. And I have been for a long time. When I have looked forward, I always saw the spectacular light bending around it. Not paying much attention to the emptiness it was all pouring into. All I had to do was let go, follow the path of least resistance, and gravity would take me right to the shiny object. But as I get closer to the void, and I truly have to face that emptiness, the reality of that future becomes clear. I look back towards anything else and see time speed up as I continue falling towards nothing. See all of possibility, of creation, beauty life pass me by.

It is haunting when you see it so clearly. But I also know that I am not alone. Gravity will pull you towards where the most energy is concentrated. If it is in the light, maybe you can just let go and you will find yourself in orbit around a warm star. But when more and more energy is concentrated in darkness, when you allow yourself to drift that is what you will fall toward. A lot of energy is pouring into dark things (consider being concrete here…)

To be honest, I am not sure if I have passed the event horizon yet. Because there is a point of no return. And that certainly is where gravity is taking me. But humans are not inert. We are reservoirs of energy, passion, desire, will, love, meaning. We will always be at the mercy of forces that far exceed us, like gravity. To be human is to fall. But we do have the power to choose what we fall towards.

But the question remains. Towards what? A question that becomes more difficult given the reality that there is so much darkness everywhere. But as I stated earlier, I am not the only one. We all exist in this environment, trying to navigate it, and many feel trapped but the same exact forces. So I need to orient myself towards others. So we can fall together. And with enough strength to find each other, and resist this condition together, we can ignite something. Stars are the tension between falling and shining. A light in the cold darkness. I will fall together with others until there is light.
