### Roadmap and Timing

The fixed dates: last day is December 5, target launch is Friday November 27 (one week of buffer), which means the album goes to the DSPs by Friday November 13. Working backward from there against the board:

- **W38–W44 (Sep 14 – Nov 1):** one track per week, seven tracks. Each production week ends with a rough mix — mixing cannot be a cold start later. Sequence tracks by song readiness (demo-stage songs first), not album order.
- **W45 (Nov 2–8):** album mixing as a cohesion pass over the rough mixes.
- **W46 (Nov 9–13):** mastering Monday–Thursday, submit to DSPs Friday 11/13.
- **W46–W48 (Nov 13–27):** rollout — codex panel, stem distribution, promo/social.
- **W48 (Nov 27):** launch. **W49:** wrap-up and retro.

Essays are drafted one per week alongside the tracks (Essay 0 this week). They land in the public repo as they're finished — the official wide release and promotion of everything happens at launch — and they don't gate the DSP deadline — any essay slippage flows into the rollout window. The music path has no such valve: it's exactly one track per week with zero slack, so a lost track week comes straight out of mixing. Two decisions baked in: media archive backup happens this week, not later (it protects the whole timeline), and the Spotify pitch happens on the short runway — pitch via Spotify for Artists as soon as the release is ingested (~Nov 13–16), which clears the 7-day minimum and guarantees Release Radar; the longer editorial lead time isn't worth pulling weeks out of a zero-slack music path. Prerequisite: claim Spotify for Artists / Apple Music for Artists profiles well before November, and deliver on a distributor with scheduled release dates.

## Retro

A good week overall, and one that changed the shape of the plan more than it executed it. On the social side, I attended my first Toastmasters meeting, which was a solid first exposure and good time getting out, and I signed up for an improv class, which I think will do a lot for thinking on my feet. Two of the protective items from the plan also got done: the media archive backup, and claiming the Spotify for Artists / Apple Music for Artists profiles.

The big strategic realization of the week: the extreme "build in public" approach doesn't work. All communication, no matter to whom, is curated — raw process just isn't legible. So the strategy shifts to making one excellent work as an attractor, even if it's just me, and moving from there. Concretely that means demo first: get an actual demo ready, then get feedback on it and iterate. This supersedes the track-per-week roadmap from this week's plan. The new phasing is September for code, October for music, November for writing — the fixed dates (launch Nov 27, last day Dec 5) still hold. The week's work already reflected the pivot: a heavy coding push on the journey experience, explore pages, the Coldness stem mixer, and production deployments. Essay 0 wasn't finished, but that was a conscious call under the new plan, not a slip — it moves into the November writing window.

The parallel realization on the social side: build the skill and experience before trying to execute on specific goals. Networking events, for example, have a formula to them. I don't love it, but instead of trying to circumvent it, the move is to learn that language first and then use it to communicate what I actually want to say once it's mastered. The framing that makes this workable: every social interaction right now is pure practice.

What didn't go well is rhythm. Sleep, workouts, the daily structure — all off this week. Big emphasis for next week is not letting that drift continue and locking it back in.
