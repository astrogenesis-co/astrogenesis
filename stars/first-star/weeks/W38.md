### Roadmap and Timing

The fixed dates: last day is December 5, target launch is Friday November 27 (one week of buffer), which means the album goes to the DSPs by Friday November 13. Working backward from there against the board:

- **W38–W44 (Sep 14 – Nov 1):** one track per week, seven tracks. Each production week ends with a rough mix — mixing cannot be a cold start later. Sequence tracks by song readiness (demo-stage songs first), not album order.
- **W45 (Nov 2–8):** album mixing as a cohesion pass over the rough mixes.
- **W46 (Nov 9–13):** mastering Monday–Thursday, submit to DSPs Friday 11/13.
- **W46–W48 (Nov 13–27):** rollout — codex panel, stem distribution, promo/social.
- **W48 (Nov 27):** launch. **W49:** wrap-up and retro.

Essays are drafted one per week alongside the tracks (Essay 0 this week). They land in the public repo as they're finished — the official wide release and promotion of everything happens at launch — and they don't gate the DSP deadline — any essay slippage flows into the rollout window. The music path has no such valve: it's exactly one track per week with zero slack, so a lost track week comes straight out of mixing. Two decisions baked in: media archive backup happens this week, not later (it protects the whole timeline), and the Spotify pitch happens on the short runway — pitch via Spotify for Artists as soon as the release is ingested (~Nov 13–16), which clears the 7-day minimum and guarantees Release Radar; the longer editorial lead time isn't worth pulling weeks out of a zero-slack music path. Prerequisite: claim Spotify for Artists / Apple Music for Artists profiles well before November, and deliver on a distributor with scheduled release dates.
