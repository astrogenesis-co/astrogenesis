_The idea is not yet written._

I remember starting this one. This was one of the many times I tried to hold myself to some sort of consistent schedule to create and release music. I tried doing one "beat" a week. I actually kind of liked the schedule I laid out, and felt like the 1st week went pretty well (Could share "Marry Me" beat).

This was the 2nd week. I was actually taking a sort of music production class at the time. I had to commute into the city for that from Long Island. Based on the schedule I was trying to maintain, the weekends were supposed to be the "ideation" phase. Then throughout the week I would sort of finalize the beat. I decided to go in early on Sunday into the city because I had some other work I wanted to handle actually. I booked a desk at a coworking space, "The Farm SoHo", and arrived a few hours before the class. I had my laptop and everything, and some time so I ended up getting into this one. Immediately I felt pretty good about the idea. It was kind of like the Higher song, where though I have trouble trying to create I would consider a more traditional pop sounding track, this one just kind of turned out that way and I didn't have to think about it much.

It's funny because I think part of what threw off my whole "beat a week" thing was that I felt like the potential for this beat exceeded the idea of a "quick beat". So I started imagining more for this track, and felt like I had two really solid potential "singles" between this and Higher. As per usual I never really got around to giving it that shot.

That being said there was some evolution from that summer. I definitely cleaned up the base foundation of the beat a bit, fixing a few things here and there such as the baseline etc. Another major shift
