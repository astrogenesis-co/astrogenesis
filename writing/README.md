# Writing

Essays in the voice of Astrogenesis itself — the scaffold, not the star.

The essays in [`stars/first-star/essays/`](../stars/first-star/essays/) are part of the first star's light: they belong
to the album, follow its narrative arc, and are finished near the end of the project.
The essays here are different. They are the scaffold thinking out loud — why stars
need to exist, what Astrogenesis is betting on, how the practice works. They can be
written and published at any point, independent of any album's progress.
