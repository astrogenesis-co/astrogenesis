About

What we do

A star is a social organization together with the work it produces. At its core is the community itself — people organized around a shared purpose, a shared practice, and the actual social experience of a group.

What radiates outward is the light: the art, writing, software, and experiences the community makes.

Astrogenesis is the scaffold around that — the infrastructure, knowledge, and practices for forming stars.
